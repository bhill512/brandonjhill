About

I’m a Colorado Springs-based remote software engineer with ten years of full-lifecycle experience. Today I ship user-facing features in a production React/TypeScript imaging platform at Planet DDS (Apteryx Imaging), and own the architecture calls, framework upgrades, and production monitoring and remediation that keep it running on Azure.

Most of what I build is infrastructure for other engineers: automated release pipelines in PowerShell and Azure DevOps, a release framework and audit trail now used across multiple teams, and a custom MCP server that unifies Jira, Confluence, and Azure DevOps behind a single queryable, agent-facing interface. I also built the data and training pipeline behind our image-quality models — extraction and de-identification through PyTorch training and evaluation.

Before Planet DDS: enterprise workflow automation at Fiserv, and mission systems in defense and space at Boeing Defense, Space & Security and Harris Space and Intelligence Systems. I’m a U.S. Army infantry veteran with combat deployments to Iraq and Afghanistan. That experience shaped how I work: steady under pressure, process-aware, and committed to doing the basics well.

Outside of work I’m in the mountains, running a local-first home server, or deep in the kinds of stories that explore systems, resilience, and long arcs — Fallout included.
